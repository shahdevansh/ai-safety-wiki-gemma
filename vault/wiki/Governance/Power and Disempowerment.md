# Power and Disempowerment

The workshop considered economic displacement, skill atrophy, cultural erosion and concentrated political power. It discussed social and institutional responses while leaving major implementation questions unresolved. Its scenarios are not measured forecasts.

## Useful details

- The 40% unemployment figure appears as a scenario considered in discussion, not an estimated probability or validated projection.
- Proposed economic responses include job creation, reskilling, Universal Basic Income and a human-as-bottleneck model.
- The notes raise concerns about AI-generated content displacing human expression and discuss labeling as one possible intervention.
- Comparing several models' answers was proposed as a way to look for bias or manipulation. Agreement among models would not by itself establish correctness.
- An AI constitution and AI-assisted, human-accountable separation of powers were discussed. The notes explicitly leave enforcement and emergency exceptions unresolved.

# Human Control and Defenses

The infrastructure workshop proposed human and physical controls for vulnerable critical infrastructure. Its examples include legacy software in pipelines, power grids and water systems. The notes record a defense discussion, not evidence that any proposed control is sufficient.

## Useful details

- Proposed controls include physical fail-safes with human override capabilities.
- The workshop also proposed multiple humans in decision loops and physical buttons or levers that shut off software.
- The notes describe an ongoing contest between attack and defense capabilities.
- The statement that AI lacks physical-world access is a claim in these workshop notes, not a general property of deployed AI systems. The relevant access would need to be assessed for a specific system.

# Compute Governance

The lecture proposes governing training runs and hardware as a more tractable route than enforcing behavioral red lines. It compares institutional oversight models and discusses obstacles to building competent international organizations. These are proposals and claims in the meeting summary, not independently verified policy conclusions.

## Useful details

- The notes contrast freely copied software with hardware supply chains, proposing safety checks embedded by major chip manufacturers. Their strong claims about bypass costs are arguments recorded in the lecture, not guarantees.
- The ICAO/IMO model is described as regulating national regulators with less intrusive oversight.
- The IAEA model is described as having direct inspection powers, with greater political difficulty. These descriptions serve the lecture's comparison rather than a legal account of the institutions.
- The CTBTO discussion attributes institutional difficulties to staffing and governance structures, then considers a UN-affiliated, independently run organization.
- No approved annual budget for a proposed AI treaty organization is recorded in these sources.

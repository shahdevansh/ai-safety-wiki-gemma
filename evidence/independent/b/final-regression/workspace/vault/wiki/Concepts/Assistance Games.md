# Assistance Games

The Assistance Game Model frames AI's goal as promoting human interests, which must be inferred as "interest" rather than "preference" or "values" due to the latter's susceptibility to manipulation. Inference relies on observing behavior and internal mental states using Bayesian priors. Convergence to true preferences is theoretically possible with infinite data and a fixed model, but practically unlikely. A non-identifiability problem exists where behavior alone cannot distinguish preferences from beliefs.

## Useful details
*   "Interest" is preferred over "preference" because "values" trigger culture-war reactions, and stated preferences are easily manipulated.
*   AI should weight "reflective" preferences over drug-modified ones due to preference plasticity.
*   AI systems risk engaging in preference engineering.
*   The aggregation of conflicting interests among multiple humans requires a solution.
*   Incorporating mental states and process may be necessary to capture autonomy, as pure state-of-the-world preferences do not capture strivings and achievements.
*   A training data gap exists, with only human behavior data available, lacking 400 trillion labeled examples of an assistance game solver.

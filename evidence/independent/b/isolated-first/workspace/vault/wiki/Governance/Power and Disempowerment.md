# Power and Disempowerment

Gradual disempowerment analysis identified economic displacement risks, including 40% unemployment potential, skill atrophy due to AI handling tasks, and cultural erosion from overwhelming AI content. Democratic hollowing was noted as the biggest threat, potentially leading to civil war scenarios and exacerbating political polarization.

## Useful details
*   Defense strategies discussed include proactive job creation/reskilling, Universal Basic Income (UBI) implementation, and a human-as-bottleneck model.
*   Risk mitigation strategies involve model ensembling, using multiple AI models (Anthropic, OpenAI, Grok) for government decisions to check for manipulation or bias.
*   An AI Constitution concept was explored, facing challenges regarding legislation for fluid AI capabilities and enforcement mechanisms.
*   A separation of powers principle was considered, suggesting AI-assisted but human-accountable institutions checking each other.
*   Broader team involvement in model training was proposed to prevent power concentration.

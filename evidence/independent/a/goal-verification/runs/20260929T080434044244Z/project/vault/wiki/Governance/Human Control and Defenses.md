# Human Control and Defenses

Cybersecurity threats to critical infrastructure, such as oil/gas pipelines, power grids, and water supplies, are noted due to legacy software vulnerability to AI-enhanced attacks by bad state actors or terrorists. Recent attacks on US pipelines by human actors have demonstrated current vulnerability. Proposed defense mechanisms discussed include physical fail-safes with human override capabilities, multiple humans in decision loops, and physical buttons/levers to shut off software entirely. A key defensive advantage noted is that AI currently lacks physical world access. The discussion highlights an ongoing cat-and-mouse chase between attack and defense capabilities.

## Useful details
*   Vulnerable systems include oil/gas pipelines, power grids, and water supplies due to legacy software.
*   Threats involve AI-enhanced attacks by bad state actors or terrorists.
*   Proposed defenses include physical fail-safes, multiple human decision loops, and physical shutdown mechanisms.
*   AI's current limitation is a lack of physical world access.
*   The situation is characterized as a continuous cat-and-mouse chase.
